# PlugZ

**Franchise Technology · Fintech · BaaS · Payments · Multi-tenant Platforms**

## Context

PlugZ is a technology ecosystem designed to support the operational and financial needs of franchise networks. My role as CTO involves connecting product strategy, software architecture and engineering execution across multiple platform domains.

## Problem space

Franchise ecosystems combine different actors, permission levels, customer journeys, commercial operations, partners and financial flows. The technology platform therefore needs to support growth without losing tenant isolation, traceability, security or operational control.

## Engineering approach

The platform is approached as a set of bounded business capabilities rather than a single monolithic workflow. Key architectural concerns include:

- multi-tenant isolation and authorization;
- commercial and CRM workflows;
- partner and enterprise integrations;
- payments and financial-service infrastructure;
- asynchronous and idempotent operations;
- observability and auditability;
- secure-by-design APIs and backoffice operations.

## Technology themes

`Python` · `FastAPI` · `PostgreSQL` · `React` · `AWS` · `Docker` · `APIs` · `RBAC` · `BaaS` · `Payments`

## My contribution

As CTO, I work across architecture, technical governance, product decisions, integrations, security and delivery. The focus is not only building features, but establishing a platform capable of supporting a growing business ecosystem.

The core repositories are private because they contain proprietary business logic and partner integrations. This case study intentionally describes the architecture and engineering scope without exposing protected implementation details.
