# DNAMark

**Biotech · Laboratory Operations · Data Workflows · Web Platform**

## Context

DNAMark applies software engineering to genetics laboratory operations, where structured data, samples, operational workflows and domain-specific processes need to coexist in a usable digital platform.

## Problem space

Laboratory software sits at the intersection of scientific information and operational execution. Data entry, sample handling, batch operations and user workflows must remain predictable and traceable while still being practical for day-to-day use.

## Engineering approach

The project focused on translating laboratory workflows into software with attention to:

- structured sample operations;
- individual and batch workflows;
- role-oriented user experiences;
- validation and predictable data handling;
- operational traceability;
- maintainable web architecture;
- deployment and source-code handoff practices.

## Engineering value

DNAMark demonstrates the ability to enter a specialized domain, model its operational language and convert those requirements into a maintainable software product.

It is also representative of an important part of my work: engineering systems for domains where software is an enabler of the core business rather than the business itself.

## Portfolio note

This case study focuses on the engineering and product challenge and intentionally excludes laboratory data, customer information and other sensitive operational details.
